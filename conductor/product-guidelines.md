# Product guidelines

Keep automatic logging simple and visible: selected destination, logging state, pending work and actionable failure messages. Preserve notes and manual changes. Offer preview for historical imports and routing changes. Treat unknown/withheld numbers explicitly; do not invent identity.

Prefer local operation, minimal permissions and deliberate disclosure to chosen destinations. Use accessible controls, readable text, localisation and platform-appropriate interactions. Mark unavailable platform capabilities honestly. Avoid implementation details in user-facing flows unless needed for a decision.

Preserve source attribution and maintain a contributor-friendly FOSS project. No branding or licence for the original CallTrack implementation is assumed. Source-derived GPL obligations must be evaluated before distribution.
